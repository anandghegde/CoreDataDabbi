# ADR-14: macOS 14, Swift 6, MIT, Observation

- **Status:** Assumed
- **Date:** 2026-09-20

## Decision

macOS 14 minimum, Swift 6 language mode, MIT licence, Observation framework for view models.

## Why

The PRD's proposals (§16 Q1, Q2). macOS 14 is needed for composite attributes and gives `@Observable`.

## Consequences

Revisit if the owner answers the PRD's open questions differently.
